Tsakonian employs a variety of prepositions to express spatial, temporal, and logical relationships. Many of these are cognates with Standard Modern Greek (SMG) but often exhibit distinct phonetic forms and syntactic behaviors.

## Prepositions

The following table lists the primary prepositions found in Tsakonian:

| Tsakonian             | English               | Notes                                                          |
| :-------------------- | :-------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **σε**                | to, in, at            | Often used in genitive replacement; coalesces with articles.   |
| **από**               | from, by              | Used for genitive replacement, especially in the plural.       |
| **μέ**                | with                  |                                                                |
| **γιά**               | for                   |                                                                |
| **κατά** (or **κά'**) | towards, according to | Often shortened to **κά'** before certain adverbs or articles. |
| **αντί**              | instead of            | From SMG _αντί_.                                               |
| **πάραξε**            | except, but           | Corresponding to SMG _πάρεξ_ or _παρά_.                        |
| **δίχως** / **χωρίς** | without               |                                                                |
| **μπρού**             | before                |                                                                |
| **μετά**              | after                 | Sometimes replaced by the participle **περάσοντα**.            |
| **ἀποκάτου**          | under                 |                                                                |
| **ἀλλάργα**           | far from              | From Italian _alla larga_.                                     |
| **ἀνάγκια**           | opposite              | Corresponding to SMG _αγνάντια_.                               |

## Morphosyntactic Features

### Periphrastic Genitive

One of the most characteristic features of Tsakonian syntax is the replacement of the morphological genitive case with a prepositional phrase consisting of **σε** or **από** followed by the accusative [[About the Genitive in Tsakonian Nouns.md|(Kisilier & Mertyris, 2018)]].

- **Example:** Use of **σε** is the most common model for genitive replacement, a feature that distinguishes Tsakonian from most other Modern Greek dialects.
- **Plurality:** In the plural, the preposition **από** is used more frequently than **σε** to indicate possession or origin.

### Article Coalescence

When the preposition **σε** precedes a definite article starting with /t/, coalescence occurs, typically resulting in an aspirated or modified sound:

- **σε + το** → **τ̒ο**
- **σε + τα** → **τ̒α**
  This process is a hallmark of Tsakonian phonology and is frequently documented in early grammars [[About the Genitive in Tsakonian Nouns.md|(Kisilier & Mertyris, 2018)]].

### Euphonic "σ'"

When prepositions such as **από**, **γιά**, or **μέ** are followed by a personal pronoun beginning with a vowel, a euphonic **σ'** (a remnant of the preposition **σε**) is often inserted between them [[Kostakis Short Grammar of Tsakonian 1951.md|(Kostakis, 1951)]].

- **Example:** _᾿Απὸ σ᾽ ἐνίου_ ("from me"), _γιὰ σ᾽ ἐτίου_ ("for you").

## Postpositions and Adverbial Use

While Tsakonian primarily uses prepositions, some adverbs and spatial markers can follow the noun or function in a way that resembles postpositional structures.

- **Spatial Adverbs:** Terms like **ἀποκάτου** (under) or **ἀπὸ τάσου** (from inside) are often used periphrastically to specify location.
- **Clitic Position:** In the Propontis dialect, the position of clitic pronouns is determined by the verb's position in the clause, sometimes appearing after the verb (enclitic) in verb-initial sentences [[Tsakonian Studies State of the Art.md|(Kisilier, 2024)]].
