Tsakonian adjectives agree with the noun they modify in **gender**, **number**, and **case** — that is, their endings change to match those of the noun. Tsakonian adjectives are classified into several types based on their gender-specific endings and declension patterns. This section is primarily based on the systematic tables provided by [[Condensed Grammar of the Tsakonian Language in Tables.md|(García Chaparro, 2023)]].

For the case and gender system used in the table headers, see [[5.1 Nouns]]. For the overall grammar structure, see the [[5. Grammar]] index.
