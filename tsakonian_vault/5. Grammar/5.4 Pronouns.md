This section covers the categorization of pronouns in Tsakonian, primarily based on the systematic tables provided by [[Condensed Grammar of the Tsakonian Language in Tables.md|(García Chaparro, 2023)]].

**Pronouns** are words that replace nouns (e.g. *he*, *she*, *it*, *them*). Tsakonian distinguishes between **strong (emphatic)** pronouns, used for emphasis or contrast, and **weak (clitic)** pronouns, which are unstressed and attach to the verb. For the rules governing how clitics are positioned relative to the verb, see [[5.9 Particles and Clitics]] and [[5.8 Syntax]].

### Personal Pronouns

### Demonstrative Pronouns

| Meaning        | Masculine (S/P) | Feminine (S/P) | Neuter (S/P)    |
| :------------- | :-------------- | :------------- | :-------------- |
| **This**       | Έντεν̇η / Έντεοι (*Énden'i / Éndei*) | Ένταν̇η / - (*Éndan'i*)     | Έγκεινι / Ένται (*Éngini / Éndai*) |
| **That**       | Ετήνε / Ετήνοι (*Etíne / Etínoi*)  | Ετήνα / - (*Etína*)      | Έκεινι / Έκεινι (*Ékini / Ékini*) |
| **That (Far)** | Άλλε / Άλοι (*Álle / Áloi*)     | Άβα / - (*Áva*)        | Άλλιου / Άβα (*Álliou / Áva*)    |
