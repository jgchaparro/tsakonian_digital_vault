# Bibliography

This bibliography lists key works, books, and dictionaries related to the Tsakonian language and culture.

## Dictionaries

- **Kostakis, T.** *Dictionary of the Tsakonian Dialect* (Λεξικό της Τσακωνικής Διαλέκτου). 3 volumes. The most comprehensive dictionary of the language, covering both Peloponnesian and Propontis Tsakonian.
- **Deffner, M.** (1923). *Lexikon der tsakonischen Mundart*. Berlin: Schade.

## Grammars and Learning Materials

- **Deffner, M.** (1881). *Grammatik der tsakonischen Mundart*. Berlin: Weidmann.
- **Scutt, C. A.** (1912–1913). "The Tsakonian Dialect." *The Annual of the British School at Athens* 19, 133–173.
- **Lekos, M.** (1920/1984). *Περί Τσακώνων και της τσακωνικής διαλέκτου*. Athens: Νότης Καραβίας.
- **Anagnostopoulos, G. P.** (1926). *Tsakonische Grammatik*. Berlin & Athens.
- **Pernot, H.** (1934). *Introduction à l'étude du dialecte tsakonien*. Paris: Les Belles Lettres.
- **Kamvysis, I.** (2020). *Για να κ̇οντούμε τα γρούσσα ναμου* (To preserve our language). Leonidio: Αρχείο Τσακωνιάς.
- **Marneris, P.** (2024). *Το βιβλίε νάμου* (Our book). Leonidio: Α Τσακωνοπαρέα.
- **García Chaparro, J.** (2023). *Condensed Grammar of the Tsakonian Language in Tables*. [[Condensed Grammar of the Tsakonian Language in Tables.md]]

## Scholarly Works

- **Newton, B.** (1972). *The Generative Interpretation of Dialect: A Study of Modern Greek Phonology*. Cambridge: Cambridge University Press.
- **Charalambopoulos, A.** (1980). *Φωνολογική ανάλυση της τσακωνικής διαλέκτου*. PhD, Aristotle University of Thessaloniki.
- **Browning, R.** (1983). *Medieval and Modern Greek*. Cambridge University Press.
- **Horrocks, J.** (2010). *Greek: A History of the Language and its Speakers*. 2nd ed. Malden, MA & Chichester: Blackwell.
- **Liosis, N.** (2017). "Tsakonian Studies: The State-of-the-Art." In *Companion to the History of the Greek Language*. [[Tsakonian Studies State of the Art.md]]
- **Kisilier, M. & Mertyris, D.** (2018). "About the Genitive in Tsakonian Nouns." *Chronicles of the Tsakonians* (Χρονικά των Τσακώνων), 23. [[About the Genitive in Tsakonian Nouns.md]]
- **Galanou, E.** (ca. 2018). "Composition in the Tsakonian Dialect." University of Patras. [[Composition in the Tsakonian Dialect.md]]
- **Lysikatos, D. S.** (n.d.). "Aspects of the Passive in Tsakonian." [[Aspects of the Passive in Tsakonian.md]]

## Digital Resources

- **Tsakonian Digital Dictionary**. First online dictionary for the Tsakonian language, featuring translations to Greek, English, and Spanish. [[About Tsakonian Digital Source.md|(García Chaparro, 2025)]]
- **Tsakonian Neural Machine Translator (NMT)**. The first AI model trained on Tsakonian, capable of translating between Tsakonian and Greek. [[About Tsakonian Digital Source.md|(García Chaparro, 2025)]]
- **Tsakonian Keyboard Extension**. An extension allowing users to write Tsakonian with Kostakis' orthography. [[About Tsakonian Digital Source.md|(García Chaparro, 2025)]]
