This bibliography lists key works on the Tsakonian language and culture. Many of these works are cited throughout the vault; those available as source notes are linked. For the history of Tsakonian scholarship, see [[1.2 History and Status]].

## Dictionaries

- **Kostakis, T.** _Dictionary of the Tsakonian Dialect_ (Λεξικό της Τσακωνικής Διαλέκτου). 3 volumes. The most comprehensive dictionary of the language, covering both Peloponnesian and Propontis Tsakonian.
- **Deffner, M.** (1923). _Lexikon der tsakonischen Mundart_. Berlin: Schade.

## Grammars and Learning Materials

- **Deffner, M.** (1881). _Grammatik der tsakonischen Mundart_. Berlin: Weidmann.
- **Scutt, C. A.** (1912–1913). "The Tsakonian Dialect." _The Annual of the British School at Athens_ 19, 133–173.
- **Lekos, M.** (1920/1984). _Περί Τσακώνων και της τσακωνικής διαλέκτου_. Athens: Νότης Καραβίας.
- **Anagnostopoulos, G. P.** (1926). _Tsakonische Grammatik_. Berlin & Athens.
- **Pernot, H.** (1934). _Introduction à l'étude du dialecte tsakonien_. Paris: Les Belles Lettres.
- **Kamvysis, I.** (2020). _Για να κ̇οντούμε τα γρούσσα ναμου_ (To preserve our language). Leonidio: Αρχείο Τσακωνιάς.
- **Marneris, P.** (2024). _Το βιβλίε νάμου_ (Our book). Leonidio: Α Τσακωνοπαρέα.
- **García Chaparro, J.** (2023). _Condensed Grammar of the Tsakonian Language in Tables_. [[Condensed Grammar of the Tsakonian Language in Tables.md]]

## Digital Resources

- **Tsakonian Digital Dictionary**. First online dictionary for the Tsakonian language, featuring translations to Greek, English, and Spanish. [[About Tsakonian Digital Source.md|(García Chaparro, 2025)]]
- **Tsakonian Neural Machine Translator (NMT)**. The first AI model trained on Tsakonian, capable of translating between Tsakonian and Greek. [[About Tsakonian Digital Source.md|(García Chaparro, 2025)]]
- **Tsakonian Keyboard Extension**. An extension allowing users to write Tsakonian with Kostakis' orthography. [[About Tsakonian Digital Source.md|(García Chaparro, 2025)]]
