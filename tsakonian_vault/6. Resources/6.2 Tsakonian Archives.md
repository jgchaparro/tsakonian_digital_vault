The **Tsakonian Archives** (_Aρχείο Τσακωνιάς_) is a cultural institution based in Leonidio, dedicated to the collection, preservation, and promotion of the historical, linguistic, and cultural heritage of Tsakonia [[Tsakonian Archives.md|(García Chaparro, 2026)]].

### History and Mission

The institution was founded by **Stylianos Sp. Merikakis**, who served as the Mayor of Leonidio from 1959 to 1978 and chaired the organization from 1954 until his death in 1980. Its mission is the preservation of the unique cultural wealth of the region. The poet and writer **Panagiotis P. Pitselas** followed him in continuing the institution's activities [[Tsakonian Archives.md|(García Chaparro, 2026)]].

On December 29, 2005, the Academy of Athens awarded a commendation to the institution's president, **Konstantinos Trochanis**, in recognition of its 50 years of social, cultural, and scientific contributions [[Tsakonian Archives.md|(García Chaparro, 2026)]].

### Publications and Events

The Archives regularly publish:
- **_Chronicles of the Tsakonians_** (_Χρονικά των Τσακώνων_): A journal containing studies on Tsakonian history, culture, and language.
- Various monographs and specialized studies.

In addition to publishing, the institution organizes:
- **International Conferences**: Held every four years, with proceedings published in the _Chronicles of the Tsakonians_.
- **Cultural Initiatives**: Such as the 1st Sculpture Symposium in Leonidio and the exhibition of significant sculptures in the courtyard of the Tsikaliotis Tower [[Tsakonian Archives.md|(García Chaparro, 2026)]].

### Connections with Other Dialects

The institution has hosted residents from Greek-speaking regions of Southern Italy (Griko speakers). It notes the evident similarities between the **Griko** and **Tsakonian** dialects [[Tsakonian Archives.md|(García Chaparro, 2026)]].
