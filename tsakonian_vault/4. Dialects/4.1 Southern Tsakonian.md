Southern Tsakonian is the most widely spoken variety of the Tsakonian language. It is primarily spoken in the following towns and villages of the Southeastern coast of the Peloponnese:

| Village                       | Tsakonian Name           |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Leonidio (Λεωνίδιο)           | Αγιε Λίδι (Agié Lídi)    |
| Prastos (Πραστός)             | Πραστέ (Prasté)          |
| Tyros (Τυρός)                 | Τερέ (Teré)              |
| Pramateftis (Πραγματευτής)    | Πραματευτά (Pramateftá)  |
| Pera Melana (Πέρα Μέλανα)     | Μέανε (Méane)            |
| Sapunakeika (Σαπουνακαίικα)   | Σαπουνάτση (Sapounátsi)  |
| Agios Andreas (Άγιος Ανδρέας) | Αγι' Αντρία (Agí Andría) |

[[Kostakis Short Grammar of Tsakonian 1951.md|(Kostakis, 1951)]]

Additionally, according to Kamvysis (2020), the following towns are also part of the Southern Tsakonian-speaking region:

- **Sampatigi (Σαμπατηγή)**
- **Vaskina (Βασκίνα)**
- **Livadi (Λιβάδι)**

Kostakis also mentions a number of smaller settlements: Sampatigi, Livadi, Paralia Tyrou, Vaskina, and Paliochora. He notes that **Plaka** (Πλάκα) had already shifted to Standard Modern Greek by his time [[Kostakis Short Grammar of Tsakonian 1951.md|(Kostakis, 1951)]].
