This article gathers freely accessible online resources for learning and engaging with Tsakonian beyond the materials in this vault.

## Panos Marneris

Panos Marneris is one of the most prolific contemporary Tsakonian writers. His website, [tsakonika.online](https://tsakonika.online), hosts a large number of bilingual Tsakonian–Greek texts across a wide range of topics, making it one of the richest freely available corpora of written Tsakonian. He also [posts regularly on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/share/1HCWzVkXTk/) and occasionally uploads videos in which he speaks the language.

Marneris writes in his own orthographic system. Before using his site, read the [[3.1 Orthography#Marneris' Orthography (2024)|Marneris' Orthography]] section in [[3.1 Orthography]] so that you can follow his texts without confusion.

> **Recommended level:** Intermediate — suitable for learners who have worked through the Grammar section and are ready to encounter natural Tsakonian prose.

## Anglo Tsakonas (YouTube)

The [Anglo Tsakonas](https://youtube.com/@anglotsakonas6711?si=qSaMrANbHUivAw5E) channel on YouTube features two high-quality videos. Each video presents Tsakonian speech with parallel Greek and English translations, making them an excellent listening resource for learners at any stage.
