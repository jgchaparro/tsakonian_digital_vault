The Tsakonian numerical system preserves many Doric features. This section is primarily based on the systematic tables provided by [[Condensed Grammar of the Tsakonian Language in Tables.md|(García Chaparro, 2023)]].

Notably, numbers 1–4 inflect for **gender** in Tsakonian (as in Ancient Greek), which means they have different forms depending on whether they modify a masculine, feminine, or neuter noun. Numbers 5 and above are invariable. The form _τσ̌ει_ (*tshei*) for '3' reflects the Tsakonian consonant change /tr/ → /ʒ/ (see [[2. Phonology]]). For Doric features preserved in the numerical system, see [[1.3 Doric heritage]].
