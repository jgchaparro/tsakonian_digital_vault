Welcome to the **Tsakonian Digital Vault**, a comprehensive digital repository dedicated to the preservation, study, and promotion of the **Tsakonian language** (*Tσακώνικα*).

## What is the Tsakonian Digital Vault?

The Tsakonian Digital Vault is an open-access platform designed to bring together linguistic data, cultural resources, and educational materials regarding one of the most unique and endangered languages of the Hellenic world. 

Tsakonian is the only living descendant of the ancient **Doric dialect**, specifically the Laconian variety spoken in ancient Sparta. Isolated for centuries in the Parnon mountains of the Peloponnese, it has preserved features that have disappeared from all other forms of Modern Greek.

## Our Mission

1.  **Preservation**: Documenting the unique phonology, grammar, and lexicon of Tsakonian.
2.  **Education**: Providing resources for those wishing to learn or teach the language.
3.  **Accessibility**: Making academic research and primary sources available to a wider audience.
4.  **Community**: Creating a space for the Tsakonian-speaking community and enthusiasts to engage with their linguistic heritage.

## How to use this Vault

You can navigate through the different sections using the explorer on the left:
- **Introduction**: Overview of the language and its history.
- **Phonology**: The unique sounds of Tsakonian.
- **Orthography**: How the language is written.
- **Grammar**: Detailed breakdown of the linguistic structure.
- **Lexicon**: A growing dictionary of Tsakonian words and expressions.
- **Resources**: A collection of external links, books, and media.
